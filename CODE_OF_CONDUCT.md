# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and
welcoming, regardless of background, identity, experience, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should communicate in good faith, focus criticism on ideas and
changes, respect differing viewpoints, and protect private or sensitive
information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
or sharing someone else's private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a conduct concern privately, email the repository maintainer at
[rsayers88@gmail.com](mailto:rsayers88@gmail.com). Do not use public issues for
sensitive reports. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with
consideration for safety and available context.

The solo maintainer may clarify expectations, remove content, limit
participation, or take other proportionate action. This is a volunteer project;
no response-time commitment is made.
